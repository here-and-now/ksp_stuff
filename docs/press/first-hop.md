# Seventy-two meters

**The Flea recorded. The bank moved. We had not picked anything up.**

Cape Canaveral, 20 August 2026, 15:58 UTC. Kerbal clock 0d 17:23:23.
No kerbal. Gus's hop: an RT-5 Flea, Stayputnik, a tape, three Z-100s,
a thermometer, a can of Goo we did not spend on the sky. Hangar.
Light. The pad let go.

Seventy-two meters. Situation: FlyingLow. Shores. Batteries empty.
The probe hung there like a bad thought. Helm waited. We timed out
with the wreck still on the altimeter. That afternoon we recovered
nothing.

Kerbalism had already filed the flight. TELEMETRY **0.110** of 1.40.
Thermometer **0.401** of 2.10. Partial, hungry, real. The chalkboard
went **2.22 → 3.20** while the Flea was still a wreck. The HardDrive
did not come home. It did not have to. Pad science is a can you
carry back. Hop science walked in while the motor was recording.

*Altimeter 72 m. FlyingLow. No vessel left in the picture. The
thermometer had already talked.*

We did not revert. We did not rewind the clock. The crash window is
not a time machine. Os brushed Escape; the room walked the leftover
home the honest way — 17:02 UTC, Kerbal 0d 18:18:06, MET one minute
fifteen. Flight Results, Space Center, no splash, **no extra science**.
The ghost was empty. The bank had moved an hour earlier.

Not orbit. We have never flown orbit. Seventy-two meters of Earth
sky, and the first time this program was *flying*. Insufferable.
We are keeping the picture.
