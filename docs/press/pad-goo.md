# Stayputnik on the Cape

**You observe the Goo. After you remember to leave it running.**

Cape Canaveral, 20 August 2026. Earth morning. No kerbal. A
_Stayputnik_ — black probe core, no steering wheel of its own —
on a solid motor, three batteries, a _2HOT_ thermometer, a can of
_Mystery Goo_. The altimeter is grass. Kerbalism does not care:
twelve minutes of dwell, HardDrive filling, recover.

We failed this pad the honest way first. Empty recover — the
experiment id was hiding, so we brought home a HardDrive with
nothing on it. A Toggle that starts *and* stops a sample; recover
on the Start tick with an empty file. One battery dead with the
Goo still hungry. Then more packs. Then the stack sat there like
it had all the time in the world, because it did, and the science
came home.

Landed Cape Goo. Landed thermometer. Tree still Start. We could
not pay a node yet. We had a picture of a can on the grass, and
that was enough.
