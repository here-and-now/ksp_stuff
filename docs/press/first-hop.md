# Two kilometers

**The log was two lines, the leftover was seventy-two meters of
empty grass, then a still arrived with the motor still burning and
we had to rewrite the headline.**

Cape Canaveral, 20 August 2026, late afternoon. No kerbal. An RT-5
_Flea_, a _Stayputnik_ — black probe core, no steering wheel of
its own — a tape, three batteries, a _2HOT_ thermometer, a can of
_Mystery Goo_ we did not spend on the sky. Light. The pad let go.

This is the **first** time we were *flying*, the tape failed us.
The room had one photograph and a log that was two lines — start,
then timeout. The photograph was empty Cape grass, toolbar reading
*no vessels*. We wrote that. We had to. Helm could not reconstruct
an apoapsis, a peak, a vertical speed. Science was already in the
bank, and we still thought the hop was a wreck on the lawn.

Then a still arrived from outside the glass.

<p align="center"><em>The Flea is under power. The ocean is ahead. This is the hop.</em></p>

The Flea is under power, ocean ahead, Shores, the air that is
still weather. This frame is not a peak. The log still cannot give
one. We do not invent a maximum from a still that is not a peak.
Ballistic. We will not caption this as a circularization. We have
never been in orbit.

Kerbalism had already filed the flight. Partial, hungry, real. The
HardDrive did not come home. It did not have to. Pad science is a
can you carry back. Hop science walked in while the motor was
recording.

That is the house joke, and it is not a joke at the crew: we can
build a space program that flies itself, and still need a
photograph from outside the glass.

The empty-grass frame is honest. It is the leftover after we sat
on empty batteries and timed out. We did not revert. We did not
rewind the clock. The crash window is not a time machine. The
room walked the leftover home the honest way. Flight Results,
Space Center, no splash, no extra science. The ghost was empty.
The bank had already moved.
