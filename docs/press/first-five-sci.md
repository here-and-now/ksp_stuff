# Five in the bank

**The Flea came home in pieces. Earth paid five for the fact of the
flight.**

Cape Canaveral, 20 August 2026, evening. No kerbal on the stack.
Jebediah Grokman on the helm. An RT-5 _Flea_, a _Stayputnik_ —
black probe core, no steering wheel of its own — a tape, three
batteries. Light. Skip the _Geiger_ — we do not have that part.
Start telemetry. The pad let go. Recovered.

No parachute. Earth does not forgive a _Flea_. The stack sat down on
Cape grass in smoke and parts, and the lab took **five** anyway.
First surviving-flight recovery. The wreck *was* the win:
Kerbalism cares that you flew, not that you arrived with dignity.

Earth paid five for the fact of the air. Telemetry while flying
over the Shores filled what it could. Ballistic. We have never
flown orbit. Tree still Start. We have not unlocked a node. We can
*pay* for one.

This is a later _Flea_. The house still of the first hop is still
the Founder's: motor lit, ocean ahead. Keep that picture. This hop
is the one that banked a workshop.
